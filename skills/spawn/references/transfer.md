Rebind existing saved-backup ownership only with recorded source-to-successor evidence; preserve paused states, entry history and checksum. On a crash between retirement and release, verify that receipt, successor readiness and schedules, then release that existing successor without recreating a chat or restarting the retired source. Report adopted destination IDs, renamed/archived sources, transferred/paused/skipped schedules and remaining blockers.

## Replay cases (offline; no live mutation)

- Busy Engineer writing ticket files: checkpoint/wait, no overlapping implementation, no forced archive.
- Two plausible Lead chats: identity question, no mutation.
- New Trader acknowledges; report replacement pending product confirmation: source retained, replacement held, no successful-transfer claim.
- Source schedule PAUSED: replacement PAUSED, no startup reactivation.
- Crash after destination rename: receipt + tool inventory reuse the same recorded destination; no duplicate creation.
- Old schedule deletion fails: replacement stays inactive; source not archived.
- Battery 8% or unknown weekly window during all: checkpoint, no Lead activation; verified migration can remain held/paused.
- Legacy Consultant idle but Researcher has another live assignment: Trader takeover can finish for accepted finance/report responsibility; Researcher retirement remains pending, no blanket archive.
- `/spawn all` with one failed bootstrap: no group release, preserve sources and partial receipt.
- No predecessor: adopt current chat held as a fresh role; no fabricated context, inherited permissions or archive target.
- Single-role current chat already registered as that role: reconcile context/schedules; no create, fork or archive.
- Another Lead already completed takeover while the human corrects behavior: clarify keep-versus-adopt before a second transfer; update instructions independently.
- `/spawn all` without a destination map: create one fresh local chat for each configured role, including AI Engineer, and rename them; keep the coordinator unchanged.
- `/spawn all` with “don’t pull over context” and a supplied progress summary: bootstrap from startup prompts and that summary; skip predecessor narratives while verifying operational cutover.
- Creation pending or failed after one destination: record returned IDs, hold the group and retry using recorded destinations; never duplicate a pending chat.

- `/spawn ai-engineer`, `ai_engineer` or `ai engineer`: adopt caller as AI Engineer without creating a chat; exact `engineer` still selects Engineer.
- Explicit “create a fresh AI Engineer chat”: create only the authorized role destination held; caller coordinates, no peer replacement or default schedule.
- AI Engineer readiness fails during `all`: retain every requested predecessor, hold every successor and activate no schedule.
- Designer exists outside roles.json: preserve that chat and its unrelated ownership; all covers only configured roles.
