### Compact-v1 profile (agreed implementation contract)
- Schema3 metadata.presentation_profile="compact-v1"; historical reports without profile retain behavior. Explicit validator --compact requires this profile for the selected report; normal validation automatically enforces a present compact-v1 profile. Unsupported nonempty profiles reject rather than silently bypass. Keep Pages historical validation compatible.
- Summary1–4 items, <=50 whitespace-delimited words per summary body; main section prose (description+item paragraphs+notes) <=1000 words total. Each confidence_reason <=40 words; scenario <=55. These are a first measurable budget aligned to Oct8 (~993 main prose words), not a universal financial policy.
- Tables with >8 rows require collapsed:true and a nonempty <=12-word details_label. Renderer respects compact-only collapse using native details/summary (closed by default); all120 audited rows remain accessible. Keep a concise score cohort summary, denominator, blockers and next recheck visible.
- Compact quote cell shows observed price, source age/time and observation qualification; full source/observed/ingested/known/cutoff times, unknown currency/session/basis/rights and data_gaps available in closed native details. Missing coverage visible without expansion. No use of provider units as verified sortable reference price.
- Three separate text+color chips: Quote (last-vs-prev-close), SMA (5-vs20) and Forecast. Bullish/bearish/neutral/unavailable and up/down/flat/Unrated must use their own payloads, with no aggregate counts changing forecast confidence. Exact method/times/parameters and missing reasons remain available on expansion; chips work with keyboard/tap without hover dependence. Quote/SMA conflicts visible through separate chips and cannot force rated forecast. Legacy forecast pill behavior preserved.
- Compact validator reconciles declared observed/missing US/MY, session-direction counts, available SMA count and verified reference count to actual rows; zero/partial inputs accepted with explicit gaps and Unrated/null targets. Reject bool-as-count and malformed values. Existing public-boundary and temporal validators continue to run.
- Every compact rated row must carry frozen forecast_basis reference close/date/source, horizon_type, bull/bear/invalidation, named forecast_model, positive observation and horizon-session counts, aware forecast_as_of<=report cutoff, coherent future target/reference date and finite ordered target/range. Reuse existing contracts without modifying protected projection module. Reject missing support or future evidence; these checks verify declared support, not provider authenticity/calibration. Do not require a minimum rated count or invent numeric output.
