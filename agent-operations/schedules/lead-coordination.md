Every 15 minutes daily from 09:00 to 05:45 next day MYT. The separate 06:00 planning report closes the working window. Resource checks first; use skills/lead/SKILL.md and the active role registry. Stay quiet on unchanged/non-actionable checks; notify meaningful progress, completion, failure or human action.

Inspect canonical plans, dirty ownership and one compact collaborator snapshot. Review returned work, then delegate bounded ready finance/reporting to Trader or accepted implementation to Engineer toward the human-selected goal. Lead writes plans/harness manifests, resolves general feasibility with temporary researchers if helpful, and owns scope/integration. Never interrupt busy chats or reassign in-flight work. Continue useful authorized work in the same run; use the next heartbeat only when blocked or safely stopped. Recheck resources before costly steps and at least every five minutes during long work.
