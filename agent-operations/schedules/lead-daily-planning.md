# Lead daily planning

Daily at 06:00 MYT, attached to verified active Lead. Resource checks first. Report previous-day 09:00 through current-day 06:00: accepted tickets, verified commit links, exact checks, work by Trader/Engineer/Lead, pending reviews, blockers and schedule interruptions. Separate reported from independently verified, committed from pushed/deployed. Save a private dated wrap-up and restart checkpoint.

Offer 3–5 concrete daily direction choices with outcome, effort, impact rationale, dependencies and recommended order. Human chooses new direction; do not automatically begin new features at 09:00. No new delegation during this reporting run. Deliver a brief truthful report even on unchanged days; record lateness or missed execution.
