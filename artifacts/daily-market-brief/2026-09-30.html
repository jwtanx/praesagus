06 · Top news

Five developments with week-ahead implications

1 · U.S. PCE came in below economists’ expectation

Reported: August headline PCE inflation was 3.4% year-over-year versus 3.7% expected; yields eased and U.S. stocks were modestly higher in early Wednesday trading, per AP. Could mean: near-term rate anxiety may cool, supporting duration-sensitive shares if yields continue lower. Counterpoint: one print does not establish a disinflation trend; Friday jobs and Monday services data can reverse the move. AP, Sep 30

2 · Treasury yields remain a key equity constraint

Reported: AP reported the 10-year yield at a 24-year high near 5.25% at Tuesday’s close; on Wednesday it eased from about 5.26% to 5.24% around the PCE report. Could mean: softer yields support growth valuations; renewed rise can overwhelm a single inflation surprise. Counterpoint: yields reflect multiple forces, not inflation alone. AP, Sep 29 close · AP, Sep 30 update

3 · Oil and Hormuz uncertainty keep an energy/inflation risk premium

Reported: AP’s Wednesday coverage cited crude near $90.42 and Brent near $97.49 in its morning snapshot, alongside uncertainty over tanker passage through Hormuz. Could mean: energy may outperform if supply concerns persist, while airlines/transport and inflation-sensitive assets may face costs. Counterpoint: no sustained supply interruption is established by this report; oil can reverse quickly. AP, Sep 30

4 · Nvidia expanded its share repurchase authorization

Reported: Nvidia added $150 billion to buybacks, leaving $235 billion authorized, according to AP. Could mean: capital returns are a supportive corporate signal, but do not establish undervaluation or AI demand. Counterpoint: buybacks do not eliminate concentration, valuation, yield, or customer-capex risks. AP report

5 · Bursa rebounded, but market breadth remained weak

Reported: FBM KLCI gained 0.43% to 1,651.17 on Sep 30 after the previous session’s sharp fall; decliners (703) outnumbered advancers (413). Could mean: bargain buying, but not a broad confirmation of recovery. Counterpoint: breadth can change quickly; seek follow-through and reclaim of the prior 1,660 area. Bernama, Sep 30
