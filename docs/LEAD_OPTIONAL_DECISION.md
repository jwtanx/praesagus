# Lead Operating Decision — 30 October 2026

## Decision and Pilot

Retain the explicitly appointed Lead as integration and independent-review owner, with short handoffs and specialist decisions inside accepted scope. The human directly selected a new Lead and resumed team execution. The prior Lead-free proposal remains an alternative if the delivery pilot fails; it is not silently enacted.

Use the next delivery as the pilot: one actual user-runnable workflow and inspectable output, no duplicate assignments, no repeated routine approvals, and exact blocker/owner/release action. Continue only if that result is demonstrated. Cost, speed and quality improvements are unmeasured until comparable observations exist. Do not restore human-stopped recurring schedules from this decision.

## Alternatives

| Mode | Value | Cost or risk | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lean Lead | One integration owner and independent acceptance | Becomes a bottleneck if every transition requires another packet | Selected: freeze scope once and shorten the hot path |
| On-demand Lead | Avoids recurring idle coordination | Unattended exceptions can wait; integration still needs an owner | Compatible with the current stopped schedules |
| Lead-free | Engineer integrates; specialists cross-review; human accepts | More Engineer integration load and human decisions | Keep as fallback if this pilot exposes the same failures |

## Roles and Handoffs

Lead freezes the usable result, paths, baseline, checks and review responsibilities; resolves conflicts and integrates. Engineer owns CLI/export, infrastructure and application code. AI Engineer owns datasets/models/numerical evaluation. Trader owns financial meaning/admission. Designer owns reading hierarchy/visual review; Engineer implements accepted design.

Each handoff contains decision, changed scope, evidence, unresolved issue and next owner. Link existing receipts rather than copying them. Normally at most two active workers; use an additional specialist only for a necessary bounded domain review while writers remain disjoint. Passing tests do not end delivery: inspect actual output or exact withholding reasons.

Human review remains required before model/prompt/risk-policy promotion, trades, material new scope or unapproved publication. This decision installs no production prompt or schedule. These coordination/report candidates remain review artifacts.

Shared inherited rules: [project coordination](../skills/praesagus-project-lead/SKILL.md), [delivery/session workflow](LEAD_WORKFLOW.md), and [resource/recovery control](../skills/lead/SKILL.md). Those linked rules define same-handle polling after timeout, rejection handling, resource gates and pause preservation. They remain authoritative; this candidate does not replace them.

If blocked, record owner, exact missing evidence and release action, then continue independent work. Keep unchanged runs quiet. Link shared financial/model/recovery rules instead of repeating them.

## Concrete Cutover and Rollback

1. Before any separately authorized replacement, save exact owned prompts/configurations/statuses and a compact checkpoint privately; verify source/destination identity and safe idle boundary.
2. The current Lead role adoption is already separately verified. The prior Lead remains archived. Do not wake paused peers merely to announce a registry map.
3. Resume only scoped slices authorized by the human's active goal. No reporting/wake schedule is restored here.
4. Before future production instruction adoption, present exact candidate text and independently reviewed replay for human review. Preserve financial/model/risk and resource semantics.
5. A separately authorized schedule move must preserve target, cadence, timezone, notification policy and human pauses. Verify inactive replacement and unique ownership before activation.
6. On failure keep replacements inactive, record last successful step and preserve original configuration. Never activate both owners.
7. Roll back only the changed owned text/configuration from its exact backup; retain human pauses and failed evidence.
8. If the pilot repeats packaging overhead, duplicate dispatch or missing integration, stop the affected transition and present the concrete evidence and alternative. Never replace a busy worker or erase a failed checkpoint.
